Falls Church Arts Unveils 68-Artist ‘Layers’ Exhibition

Falls Church Arts will showcase work by 68 regional artists in “Layers,” an all-media exhibition opening Aug. 16 at its gallery at 700-B W. Broad St. in Falls Church.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Falls Church
Section
Culture
Published
August 5, 2025
Topics
culture, falls church, arts, environment

Article Text

Falls Church Arts will showcase work by 68 regional artists in “Layers,” an all-media exhibition opening Aug. 16 at its gallery at 700-B W. Broad St. in Falls Church.

The exhibition runs through Sept. 28 and explores how layering can convey memory, transformation, identity and emotion. A free meet-the-artists reception is scheduled from 7 to 9 p.m. Saturday, Aug. 16, when the Juror’s Choice Award will also be announced.

Artists from Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland contributed photography, paintings, collages, jewelry, fiber art, found-object pieces and sculptures. The show includes work by both emerging and established artists.

Juror Tricia Ratliff said the collection encourages visitors to look beneath the surface and find beauty in complexity. Ratliff, who juried the 2025 Falls Church Arts Plein Air Festival, is trained in classical and contemporary art methods. Her work has been exhibited and collected internationally, and she spent a decade teaching painting and drawing in a classical atelier.

Ratliff continues to lead workshops and has judged numerous exhibitions and competitions. Her own art draws heavily from nature, renewal and resilience, often inspired by time spent painting in unfamiliar places and learning about their communities.

Visitors may vote for the exhibition’s People’s Choice Award at the gallery through Sept. 12.

Participating artists are Jean Marie Ahanda, Lisbet Axell, Kay Bailey, John Ballou, Cheryl Ann Bearss, Blanca Belisario, Noreen Brunini, Megan Day, Anna Demovidova, Kristine DeNinno, Jennifer Dreyfus, Patti Durbin, Maluchi Enyi, Sharon Fields, Susan Fine, Mara Flynn, Marilyn Geldzahler, Patricia Giovannoni, Anjori Halder, James Hengst, Beth Higgins, Dan Hildt, Susan Hillson, Sydney Hunter, Lizzy Javier, Steve Jennings, Susanne Kamalieh, Martha Kaplan, Gerda Lane, Jessica Lehman, Sandra Lewin, Julia Licholai, Donna Liguria, Trisha Love, Linda Lowery, Russ Mardon, Neha Mathur, Erasto Curtis Matthews, Stephanie McGee, Judith Mensh, Jessica Mickey, Cynthia K Miller, Sasha More, Nan Morrison, Nicki Mulligan, Jenny Newberry, Hazel Nguyen, Yasuo Oda, Barbara Osborne-Harris, Sandi Parker, Nancy Patrick, Erin Peterson-Fleming, Laura Ramírez-Drain, Elisabeth Rhyne, Andrea Salzman, Susan Sanders, Cynthia Schoeppel, Todd Schvaneveldt, Jenny Sewell, Janet Sifers, Celia Slater, Gillian Stern, Terry Svat, Carina Tenaglia, Lara Tiller, Suzanne Updike, Bob Wentworth and Amy Woodhouse.

Admission is free. Gallery hours are 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Tuesdays through Fridays, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturdays and 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Sundays. The artwork will also be displayed online, and all pieces may be purchased through the gallery or its website.
