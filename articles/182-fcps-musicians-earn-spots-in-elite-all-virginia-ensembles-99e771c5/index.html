182 FCPS Musicians Earn Spots in Elite All-Virginia Ensembles

A rigorous statewide audition process has earned 182 Fairfax County Public Schools students places in the 2025 All-Virginia Band, Chorus and Orchestra.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
McLean
Section
Schools
Published
April 8, 2025
Topics
schools, mclean, arts

Article Text

A rigorous statewide audition process has earned 182 Fairfax County Public Schools students places in the 2025 All-Virginia Band, Chorus and Orchestra.

The students will join other accomplished young musicians from across Virginia for the April 24-26 event in Richmond. Participants will rehearse under nationally recognized conductors before performing together at the Richmond Convention Center.

The concerts are free and open to the public. The published schedule lists performances on Saturday, April 24, from 11 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
