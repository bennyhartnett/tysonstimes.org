Restaurant Week Brings Three-Course Deals to Tysons and Reston

Winter Restaurant Week returns Jan. 19-25, 2026, with specially priced three-course meals at restaurants across Tysons, Reston and nearby Northern Virginia communities.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Tysons
Section
Business
Published
January 9, 2026
Topics
business, tysons, metro

Article Text

Winter Restaurant Week returns Jan. 19-25, 2026, with specially priced three-course meals at restaurants across Tysons, Reston and nearby Northern Virginia communities.

Dinner menus are available at $40, $55 or $65, while brunch and lunch options cost $25 or $35. Some restaurants also offer wine or cocktail pairings, outdoor seating and delivery, with availability varying by location.

The Restaurant Association Metropolitan Washington presents Metropolitan Washington Restaurant Week twice each year.

Participating restaurants

Tysons and McLean

  • 801 Chophouse — Tysons Corner, 8484 Westpark Drive, McLean: $35 lunch and $65 dinner.
  • Agora Tysons, 7911 Westpark Drive, Tysons: $35 brunch, $35 lunch and $65 dinner. Wine or cocktail pairings are offered.
  • American Prime, 1420 Spring Hill Road, McLean: $25 lunch and $65 dinner. Pairings and outdoor dining are available.
  • The Capital Grille — Tysons, 1861 International Drive, McLean: $65 dinner.
  • Chima Steakhouse, 8010 Towers Crescent Drive, Suite 100, Tysons Corner: $35 lunch, plus $40 or $55 dinner.
  • Circa at The Boro, 1675 Silver Hill Drive, Tysons: $35 lunch, plus $55 or $65 dinner.
  • Founding Farmers — Tysons, 1800 Tysons Blvd. at Galleria Drive, McLean: $25 lunch and a dinner option listed at $04. Delivery is available through Uber Eats and the restaurant’s website.
  • Ingle Korean Steakhouse, 8369 Leesburg Pike, Suite 120, Vienna: $65 dinner.
  • J. Gilbert’s — Wood Fired Steaks & Seafood, 6930 Old Dominion Drive, McLean: $35 lunch and $55 dinner.
  • Joon, 8045 Leesburg Pike, Suite A, Vienna: $35 brunch, $35 lunch and $65 dinner. Pairings, outdoor dining and delivery are available.
  • North Italia Tysons, 1651 Boro Place, McLean: $25 brunch, $25 lunch and $55 dinner. Pairings are offered.
  • Ometeo, 1640 Capital One Drive, Tysons: $35 brunch, $35 lunch, and dinner for $40 or $55. Pairings and delivery are available.

Reston

  • Big Buns Reston Station, 1908 Reston Metro Plaza: $25 lunch. Outdoor dining and delivery are available.
  • Founding Farmers — Reston, 1904 Reston Metro Plaza Drive: $25 lunch and a dinner option listed at $04. Delivery is available through Uber Eats and the restaurant’s website.
  • Heirloom, 1871 Fountain Drive, Suite 300: dinner for $55 or $65.
  • Morton’s The Steakhouse — Reston, 11956 Market Street: $35 lunch and $55 dinner.
  • North Italia Reston, 11898 Market St.: $25 brunch, $25 lunch and $55 dinner. Pairings are offered.
  • PassionFish, 11960 Democracy Drive: $35 brunch, $35 lunch and $55 dinner.
  • Pisco Y Nazca Ceviche Gastrobar — Reston, 1871 Explorer Street: $35 brunch, $25 lunch and $40 dinner.

Falls Church

  • 2941, 2941 Fairview Park Drive: $65 dinner. Wine or cocktail pairings are offered.
  • Chasin’s Tails, 944 West Broad: $65 dinner.
  • Ellie Bird, 125 Founders Ave.: $65 dinner. Outdoor dining is available.
  • Fava Pot, 7393-D Lee Highway: $35 lunch and $55 dinner. Pairings and outdoor dining are available.
  • Idylwood Grill & Wine Bar, 2190 Pimmit Drive, Unit B: $25 brunch, $35 lunch and $55 dinner. Delivery is available through DoorDash, Grubhub and Uber Eats.
  • Seoul Prime, 106 Founders Ave.: $65 dinner.
  • TRIO Grill, 8100 Lee Highway: dinner for $55 or $65. Outdoor dining is available.

Fairfax

  • Alta Strada Mosaic, 2911 District Avenue: $35 brunch, $35 lunch and $40 dinner. Outdoor dining is available.
  • B Side, 8298 Glass Alley: $25 brunch and $40 dinner.

  • The Capital Grille — Fairfax, 12169 Fair Lakes Promenade: $65 dinner.

Vienna

Sterling and Dulles

  • Local Provisions, 46286 Cranston St., Sterling: $35 brunch, $35 lunch and $55 dinner.

Ashburn

Arlington
