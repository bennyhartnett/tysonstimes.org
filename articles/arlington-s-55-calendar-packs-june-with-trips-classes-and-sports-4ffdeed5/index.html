Arlington’s 55+ Calendar Packs June With Trips, Classes and Sports

Arlington’s 55+ Programs are offering a full schedule of social gatherings, fitness activities, cultural programs, classes and regional excursions in June.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Arlington
Section
Local
Published
June 4, 2025
Topics
local, arlington, metro, arts

Article Text

Arlington’s 55+ Programs are offering a full schedule of social gatherings, fitness activities, cultural programs, classes and regional excursions in June.

Most activities take place in person at Arlington’s 55+ Centers. Participation generally requires a 55+ Pass, with annual fees starting at $25. Registration is available at registration.arlingtonva.us or by calling 703-228-4747.

Weekly drop-in activities

Coffee or Tea and Thee offers time to socialize at Lubber Run 55+ Center from 9 to 10 a.m. Mondays and Thursdays.

Volleyball for all skill levels meets from 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. Tuesdays at Langston-Brown 55+ Center and from 2 to 4 p.m. Thursdays at Lubber Run.

Volunteer Suzie Pratts leads an introductory line-dancing class Fridays at Aurora Hills 55+ Center. Beginners can learn the box step, quarter turn and grapevine.

Cribbage is played from 10 a.m. to noon Fridays at Walter Reed 55+ Center. New players are welcome to learn the card game.

Water walking and lap swimming are available at Long Bridge Aquatics Center, 333 Long Bridge Drive, from 8 to 10 a.m. Mondays and 1 to 3 p.m. Wednesdays. The sessions do not include instruction. Participants need an aquatics center membership or must pay the daily admission fee.

Volunteer-led Tai Chi practice sessions are held from 2 to 2:45 p.m. Mondays at Aurora Hills and from 1 to 2:15 p.m. Tuesdays and Fridays at Walter Reed. No instruction is provided.

Senior public skating runs from 8:40 to 9:40 a.m. Mondays at MedStar Capitals Iceplex, accessible through roof-level parking at Ballston Quarter. Skate rental costs $1. Advance registration is required through the Senior Public Skate section of medstarcapitalsiceplex.com.

Pickleball is available at Walter Reed from 8:15 a.m. to 4 p.m. Mondays and Fridays, 12:30 to 4 p.m. Tuesdays and Thursdays, and 2:15 to 4 p.m. Wednesdays. Arlington Mill offers pickleball from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays and noon to 3 p.m. Tuesdays and Thursdays. A 55+ Pass is required.

Women’s drop-in basketball takes place from 7:15 to 8:45 p.m. Wednesdays at Langston-Brown.

Special programs and outings

Opera enthusiasts can watch and discuss Johann Strauss’ “Die Fledermaus” at 1:30 p.m. Wednesday, June 4, at Lubber Run. George Cecchetti will provide professional commentary during the Metropolitan Opera on Demand presentation. Registration is #914300-01.

A virtual trivia challenge is scheduled for 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Thursday, June 5. Registration is #914600-13.

Langston-Brown will hold a fitness-equipment orientation at 1:30 p.m. June 5, teaching participants how to use the center’s machines properly. Registration is #914100-19.

Pride Dance Bingo, which combines cardio dancing with bingo, begins at 1 p.m. June 5 at Walter Reed. Registration is #914802-01.

Musicians of every skill level can bring guitars, fiddles, banjos, mandolins and dulcimers to an old-time and string-band jam at 5 p.m. June 5 at Lubber Run. The session will focus on Appalachian old-time, pre-bluegrass and string-band music. Registration is #914304-14.

The 55+ Travel group will visit Tea with Mrs. B in Falls Church on Friday, June 6. The outing costs $86 for Arlington residents and $99 for nonresidents. Registration is #902506-01.

A Senior Housing Fair will cover affordable housing, home modifications and other resources from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. June 6 at Lubber Run. The drop-in event is open to all ages, and no 55+ Pass is required.

The Arlington Spellbinders, an oral-storytelling group, will meet from 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. June 6 at Langston-Brown. Newcomers may drop in.

A June 7 Secret Garden tour will take the travel group through tucked-away gardens along Annapolis’ historic streets. The trip costs $65 for Arlington residents and $75 for nonresidents. Registration is #902506-02.

Virginia Cooperative Extension Master Food Volunteers will present a food-safety and side-dish cooking demonstration at 11:30 a.m. Monday, June 9, at Walter Reed. Registration is #914501-01.

Scale Down, a weight-loss support group featuring private weigh-ins and programs, meets at 1:30 p.m. June 9 and June 23 at Langston-Brown. Both sessions are drop-in.

Matthew Quinn of Quinn’s Action Galleries will explain how to sort family treasures and navigate estate sales and auctions at 1:30 p.m. June 9 at Langston-Brown. Registration for “Keep the Best and Get Rid of the Rest” is #914400-08.

Certified coach and facilitator Sallie Wiley will lead “Navigating Change and Uncertainty” at 1 p.m. June 9 at Lubber Run. The program focuses on making sound choices and identifying new opportunities. Registration is #914400-09.

Art historian Joan Hart will discuss the Group of Seven and its striking Canadian landscapes at 1 p.m. June 9 at Aurora Hills. Registration for the Canadian visionary artists presentation is #914302-02.

The travel group will cruise across the Chesapeake Bay to St. Michaels on June 9. The $120 resident and $138 nonresident prices include admission to the Chesapeake Bay Maritime Museum. Participants will explore the historic village, shops and other sites, with meals purchased separately. Registration is #902506-13.

Jessica Kaplan, editor of the Arlington Historical Society Magazine, will present a program about memorializing enslaved people in Arlington’s history at 1 p.m. Tuesday, June 10, at Arlington Mill. Registration is #914400-16.

Genealogist Eileen Bogdanoff will introduce primary-source research, including the use of Ancestry.com, from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. June 10 at Lubber Run. Registration for Genealogy 101 is #914402-01.

Virginia Cooperative Extension Master Food Volunteers will also offer a pasta-cooking demonstration at 10 a.m. June 10 at Lubber Run. Registration is #914501-02.

An Arlington County librarian will highlight beach-themed fiction and nonfiction at 1 p.m. Wednesday, June 11, at Aurora Hills. Registration for “To the Beach With Books” is #914400-24.

History roundtable participants will meet virtually at 11:15 a.m. June 11 to discuss the historical impact of insects and diseases. Registration is #914402-04.

A program exploring the history of English and language more broadly begins at 10:30 a.m. June 11. Participants may attend at Lubber Run under registration #914402-12 or join virtually under #914402-13.

The travel group will take an LGBTQ+ walking tour of the Congressional Cemetery in Washington, D.C., on June 11. The outing is not ADA compliant and costs $21 for Arlington residents and $24 for nonresidents. Registration is #902506-04.

Ashley Gomez, director of Lubber Run 55+ Center, will begin a virtual tour of Spanish-speaking South America with Uruguay and Paraguay at 1 p.m. Thursday, June 12. Registration is #914400-28.

A beginner quilling class at 1:30 p.m. June 12 at Lubber Run will teach participants to create raised images from coiled, one-eighth-inch-wide paper strips. Supplies are included in the $18 fee. Registration is #914310-01.

Karaoke Night starts at 6 p.m. June 12 at Lubber Run. Registration is #914304-20.

Walter Reed will introduce a drop-in Spades gathering at 1 p.m. Friday, June 13. Beginners are welcome to learn the partnership-based trick-taking card game.
