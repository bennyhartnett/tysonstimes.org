2026 Farmers Markets Return Across Northern Virginia

By Tysons Times Staff

Article Text

Warmer weather is bringing farmers markets back across Northern Virginia, with vendors offering locally produced fruits, vegetables, meats, eggs, cheeses, plants, baked goods and other products. Most vendors sell items they have grown or made themselves.

Here is the 2026 market schedule by day of the week.

Wednesday

Oakmont Farmers Market May 6–October 28, 8 a.m.–noon Oakmont Rec Center 3200 Jermantown Road, Oakton

FRESHFARM Reston January 7–December 30, 3–7 p.m. Year-round market Winter hours: 2–6 p.m., November 2026–March 2027 St. John Neumann Catholic Church 11900 Lawyers Road, Reston

Thursday

Herndon Farmers Market May 7–October 29, 8 a.m.–noon 765 Lynn St., Herndon

Friday

McLean Farmers Market May 1–October 30, 8 a.m.–noon Closed May 15 for the McLean Day event Lewinsville Park 1659 Chain Bridge Road, McLean

Saturday

Falls Church Farmers Market April–December, 8 a.m.–noon January–March, 9 a.m.–noon City Hall parking lot 300 Park Ave., Falls Church

Leesburg Saturday Market May–October, 8 a.m.–noon November–April, 9 a.m.–noon Virginia Village Shopping Center 30 Catoctin Circle SE, Leesburg

Reston Farmers Market April 25–November 21, 8 a.m.–noon Lake Anne Village Center 1609 Washington Plaza N., Reston

Vienna Farmers Market May–October, 8 a.m.–noon Vienna Community Center 120 Cherry St. SE, Vienna

Fairfax Saturday Farmers’ Market May 9–October 31, 9 a.m.–1 p.m. 10500 Page Ave., Fairfax The market is one block from West and Main streets, behind Wells Fargo Bank and beside the Historic Fairfax County Courthouse.

EatLoco Ashbrook Farmers Market Year-round, 9 a.m.–1 p.m. 44505 Atwater Drive, Ashburn

Great Falls Farmers Market 10 a.m.–1:30 p.m. 756 Walker Road, Great Falls

Sunday

Cascades Farmers Market May 3–November 1, 9 a.m.–noon 21060 Whitfield Drive, Sterling The market is held in the Senior Center parking lot.

FRESHFARM Mosaic January 4–December 27, 9 a.m.–2 p.m. Year-round market Winter hours: 10 a.m.–2 p.m., January–March 2027 2920 District Ave., Fairfax The market is on District Avenue between Merrifield Town Center and Strawberry Lane.

EatLoco Reston at Halley Rise Market March 29–November 22, 9 a.m.–1 p.m. Halley Rise 2001 Edmund Halley Drive, Reston

EatLoco Brambleton Market Year-round, 9 a.m.–1 p.m. Brambleton Town Center 42395 Ryan Road, Brambleton
