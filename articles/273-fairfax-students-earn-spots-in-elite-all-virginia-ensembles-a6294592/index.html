273 Fairfax Students Earn Spots in Elite All-Virginia Ensembles

A total of 273 Fairfax County Public Schools students have earned places in prestigious statewide music ensembles following a competitive audition process.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Fairfax
Section
Schools
Published
April 9, 2026
Topics
schools, fairfax, arts

Article Text

A total of 273 Fairfax County Public Schools students have earned places in prestigious statewide music ensembles following a competitive audition process.

The selections include 217 high school musicians who will participate in the 2026 All-Virginia Band, Chorus and Orchestra event from April 16-18. Another 56 students will perform during the All-Virginia Middle School Chorus event on April 16-17.

Students will rehearse alongside musicians from across Virginia under nationally recognized conductors. The resulting concerts are free and open to the public.

High school performances are scheduled for 11 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Saturday, April 18, at the Richmond Convention Center. The middle school chorus concerts will begin at 6:45 p.m. Friday, April 17, at the same venue.
