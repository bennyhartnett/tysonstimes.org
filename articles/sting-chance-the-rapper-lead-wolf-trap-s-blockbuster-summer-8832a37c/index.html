Sting, Chance the Rapper Lead Wolf Trap’s Blockbuster Summer

Wolf Trap’s 2026 summer season will bring Sting, Chance the Rapper, Melissa Etheridge and a wide-ranging celebration of America’s 250th anniversary to Vienna.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Vienna
Section
Culture
Published
February 11, 2026
Topics
culture, vienna, arts, sports

Article Text

Wolf Trap’s 2026 summer season will bring Sting, Chance the Rapper, Melissa Etheridge and a wide-ranging celebration of America’s 250th anniversary to Vienna.

Sting will launch the concert season with three performances from May 21 through May 23. The newly announced lineup stretches into September and spans rock, pop, classical music, opera, dance and film screenings with live musical accompaniment.

Several events are tied to the America250 initiative, including “American Mosaic,” a new musical and visual work performed by the National Symphony Orchestra. The Beach Boys and the U.S. Marine Band will also take part in anniversary programming.

Tickets go on sale Friday, Feb. 27, at 10 a.m., with presales available to Wolf Trap members.
