Fairfax Restaurant Week Deals Expand to More Than 30 Local Spots

More than three dozen Fairfax County restaurants will roll out specially priced meals when Winter Restaurant Week returns Monday, Jan. 27.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Fairfax
Section
Business
Published
January 24, 2025
Topics
business, fairfax, metro, public safety

Article Text

More than three dozen Fairfax County restaurants will roll out specially priced meals when Winter Restaurant Week returns Monday, Jan. 27.

The promotion runs through Sunday, Feb. 2, bringing multi-course brunch, lunch and dinner menus to restaurants across Fairfax County and the wider D.C. region. Some establishments will also offer wine, cocktail or other drink pairings, typically for an additional charge.

Several restaurants owned by Reston-based Thompson Restaurants—including Matchbox, Big Buns, Makers Union, and Milk & Honey—will extend their promotional menus through Feb. 9.

Merrifield

  • 2941 Restaurant: Three-course dinner for $65, with a paired wine or cocktail. 2941 Fairview Park Drive.
  • Alta Strada Mosaic: $35 lunch and $65 dinner, with paired wine or cocktail. 2911 District Avenue, Unit 150.
  • B Side: $25 brunch and $40 dinner, including a starter, main course and dessert. 8298 Glass Alley, Unit 110.
  • Kirby Club: $35 three-course brunch with coffee or hot tea; $25 three-course lunch; and three-course dinners for $40 or $55. 2911 District Avenue, Unit 140.
  • Matchbox Pizza: $25 lunch and $40 dinner with an appetizer, entree and dessert, plus suggested wine pairings. Locations in the Mosaic District, McLean and Reston Station.
  • Trio Grill: Dinner menus for $55 or $65. 8100 Lee Highway.

  • Agora Tysons: Three-course lunch with dessert for $35; four-course dinner with dessert for $65; and a $35 bottomless brunch available only Feb. 1 and 2. 7911 Westpark Drive.
  • American Prime: $25 lunch and $65 dinner, each with an appetizer, entree and dessert. Wine and cocktail pairings cost extra. 1420 Spring Hill Road.
  • Chima Steakhouse: $35 three-course weekend lunch and a choice of $40 or $55 dinner menus. 8010 Towers Crescent Plaza.
  • Circa at The Boro: $35 three-course lunch and $55 or $66 dinners with two courses and dessert. 1675 Silver Hill Drive.
  • Earls Kitchen and Bar: $35 lunch and $40 dinner, with paired wine and cocktails. 7902 Tysons One Place.
  • El Bebe at The Boro: $25 lunch and $40 dinner. 8354 Broad Street.
  • Härth: $55 dinner with flatbread and a choice of starter, entree and dessert. 7920 Jones Branch Drive.
  • Ingle Korean Steakhouse: $65 dinner featuring a welcome dish, sides, appetizer, savory entree and dessert. 8369 Leesburg Pike, Suite A.
  • Joon: Three-course brunch and lunch menus for $35 and a three-course dinner for $65. 8045 Leesburg Pike, Suite 120.
  • Ometeo: Brunch and lunch for $35, with dinner options for $40 or $55. 1640 Capital One Drive.
  • The Capital Grille: $65 dinner with a choice of salad or clam chowder, followed by an entree, side and dessert. Locations in Tysons and Fair Lakes.
  • Wildfire: $35 lunch and $55 dinner with an appetizer, main course and dessert. 2001 International Drive.
  • Wren: Dinner menus for $40 or $55.

Fairfax City

  • Bellissimo Restaurant: Lunch menus for $25 or $35 and dinners for $40 or $65. 10403 Main Street.
  • Hamrock’s Restaurant: $25 brunch, $25 lunch and $40 dinner. 3950 Chain Bridge Road.
  • Kelly’s Oyster House and Bar: $25 brunch, $25 lunch and $40 dinner with an appetizer, entree and dessert. 4069 Chain Bridge Road.
  • Milk & Honey: $35 lunch and $55 dinner. Locations in Fairfax City and Belle Haven.

  • PassionFish: $35 brunch offered only Sunday, Feb. 2; $35 lunch; and $55 dinner with a choice of appetizer, entree and dessert. 11960 Democracy Drive.
  • Pisco Y Nazca Ceviche Gastrobar: $35 brunch with a mimosa, $25 lunch and $40 dinner. 1871 Explorer Street.
  • The Melting Pot: $55 dinner menu. 11730 Plaza America Drive, Suite 100.
