Fairfax County Nearly Landed the Sphere as Major Projects Take Shape

Fairfax County was reportedly considered for the Sphere’s second location before the Las Vegas concert venue selected National Harbor in Maryland in January.

Article Text

Fairfax County was reportedly considered for the Sphere’s second location before the Las Vegas concert venue selected National Harbor in Maryland in January.

State Sen. Scott Surovell referenced the discussions while responding to Gov. Abigail Spanberger’s veto of his bill that would have permitted a casino in Fairfax County. The Fairfax County Economic Development Authority declined to say whether it knew about the potential Sphere deal.

The Trump administration has submitted architectural plans for a proposed 250-foot triumphal arch at Memorial Circle, prompting strong opposition from Arlington’s congressional representative. Renderings depict a neoclassical stone structure topped by a 60-foot gilded winged figure that President Trump has called Lady Liberty, accompanied by two smaller golden eagles.

Bill Mann, a longtime Associated Press reporter and editor, died Thursday in Reston at age 83, his family said. During a career spanning nearly five decades, Mann covered the Philippines, Cairo, India, Scandinavia and Washington, D.C.

Virginia electricity bills have risen 14.5% since 2022 and doubled over the past 25 years. A growing share of the cost comes from rate adjustment clauses, commonly called riders, that fund various power and clean-energy projects but are not itemized clearly on Dominion Energy customer bills.

Herndon-based defense contractor HawkEye 360 Inc. filed plans for an initial public offering after several major capital raises. The company had not disclosed how many shares it would offer, the expected price or a timeline, but said Friday, April 10, that it plans to trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker “HAWK.”

Talent Capital AI, a regional job-search platform launched in October to assist displaced federal workers, has attracted more than 100,000 unique users. D.C. Deputy Secretary for Education Paul Kihn told the Metropolitan Washington Council of Governments that the service is helping residents prepare for changes in the region’s job market.

Annandale’s first Asian Night Market drew a large crowd to the ACCA Child Development Center parking lot on Columbia Pike on April 11. Presented by Neon District DMV, the event featured 32 food vendors—most serving Thai specialties—and 32 non-food vendors.

Plans are also advancing for national and regional celebrations marking the country’s 250th anniversary. Fairfax County 250th Commission Chair Gretchen Bulova, who is now a state delegate, said Virginia’s preparations will include mobile museums and events stretching from Mount Vernon to Monticello ahead of July 4.

Monday’s forecast calls for partly sunny skies, a high near 83 degrees and southwest winds around 14 mph, with gusts reaching 26 mph. The night should be partly cloudy, with a low near 63 and southwest winds of 3 to 7 mph.
