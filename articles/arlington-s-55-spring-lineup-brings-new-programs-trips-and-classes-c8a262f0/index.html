Arlington’s 55+ Spring Lineup Brings New Programs, Trips and Classes

Arlington’s Office of 55+ Programs is welcoming spring with exercise classes, educational talks, cultural outings and a new Memory Care Café.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Arlington
Section
Local
Published
March 18, 2025
Topics
local, arlington, public safety, business

Article Text

Arlington’s Office of 55+ Programs is welcoming spring with exercise classes, educational talks, cultural outings and a new Memory Care Café.

Programs are held in person at 55+ Centers unless identified as virtual. Participation generally requires a 55+ Pass, available for an annual fee starting at $20. Register at registration.arlingtonva.us or call 703-228-4747.

Stay Active and Independent for Life classes will focus on strength, balance and overall fitness. The free sessions meet Mondays and Thursdays from March 3 through May 22 at 1 p.m. at Lubber Run 55+ Center, registration #913502-01. A second series meets Tuesdays and Thursdays from March 4 through May 22 at 10:30 a.m. at Arlington Mill 55+ Center, registration #913502-02.

Wednesday, March 19

Elder law attorney Ed Zetlin will explain living trusts and their benefits during a virtual presentation at 11 a.m. Registration #913404-03.

Arlington Park Naturalist Bobbi Farley will discuss animals emerging in spring and lead a hike beginning at 11 a.m. at Aurora Hills 55+ Center. Walking shoes are recommended. Registration #913106-15.

A video presentation of “Siegfried,” the third opera in Richard Wagner’s “Ring Cycle,” will begin at 1:30 p.m. at Lubber Run 55+ Center. The New York Met performance will include professional commentary from George Cecchetti. Registration #913300-02.

Cory Cox, a 55+ operations supervisor, will present a program about the women’s suffrage movement at 2 p.m. at Aurora Hills 55+ Center. Registration #913400-30.

Valerie Welsh will perform Golden Oldies from the early 1960s during a Piano Lounge and Sing-Along at 4 p.m. at Lubber Run 55+ Center. Lyrics will be provided. Registration #913304-23.

Author Sushmita Mazumdar will share women’s culinary stories at 6 p.m. at Arlington Mill 55+ Center as part of Women’s History Month. Her book, “Columbia Pike Recipes for Recovery,” explores how food connected a community during a time of need. Registration #913400-31.

Thursday, March 20

Tech specialist Nick Englund will offer tips for using Uber and Lyft during a virtual program at 10 a.m. Registration #913403-02.

Virginia Cooperative Extension Master Food Volunteers will demonstrate heart-healthy recipes at 11 a.m. at Langston-Brown 55+ Center. Registration #913501-01.

A new Memory Care Café will meet from 1:30 to 3 p.m. at Walter Reed 55+ Center. Arlington Adult Day Program and library staff will lead dementia-friendly activities for people with dementia and their family caregivers.

The café is open to all ages, and a 55+ Pass is not required. Participants with dementia must be accompanied by a caregiver. Register by calling Zoe Mann at 703-228-5193 or emailing zmann@arlingtonva.us.

March 24-25

On Monday, March 24, the 55+ Travel group will visit the National Gallery of Art in Washington, D.C., for the retrospective “Elizabeth Catlett: A Black Revolutionary Artist and All That It Implies,” highlighting the artist’s impact. The trip costs $11 for Arlington residents and $13 for nonresidents. Registration #902503-09.

Arlington Park Naturalist Simon Willig will lead an early-wildflower walk at 10 a.m. Tuesday, March 25, along the wooded trails at Gulf Branch Nature Center, 3608 Military Road. The program will examine the plants’ ecology. Registration #913106-25.

Karen Close and Candee Currie of Century 21 New Millennium Real Estate will share strategies for preparing a home for sale in the current market at 11 a.m. at Langston-Brown 55+ Center. Registration #913404-04.

A matinee screening of the 2016 PG-rated film “Hidden Figures” will begin at 1 p.m. at Lubber Run 55+ Center. Registration #913804-01.

Wednesday, March 26

The 55+ Travel group will have afternoon tea at Reynolds Tavern Tea Room in Annapolis, Maryland. The trip costs $80 for Arlington residents and $92 for nonresidents. Registration #902503-10.

Travel specialist Susan Court will discuss her experiences exploring Alsace, the Germanic region of France, at 1 p.m. at Aurora Hills 55+ Center. Registration #913400-28.

Cherrydale Library staff will visit Langston-Brown 55+ Center at 1:30 p.m. Participants can apply for an Arlington Public Library card, learn how to access free e-books and audiobooks, and check out titles for spring reading. Registration #913400-03.

A new Spring Supper Club will begin at 6 p.m. at Arlington Mill 55+ Center. Participants should bring their own dinners; beverages and dessert will be supplied. Registration #913899-03.

Thursday, March 27

Community Arts Programmer Jennifer Droblyen will lead a virtual “Artist Cookbook” program at 9 a.m., sharing techniques for creating and using art materials made from household items. Registration #913302-06.

VHC Health Auxiliary Vice President Mary Gleason will explain hospital volunteer opportunities and the process for becoming a volunteer at 11 a.m. at Langston-Brown 55+ Center. Registration #913400-04.

Dwight Rodgers of Encore Learning will lead a virtual history discussion group at 1 p.m. Topics will be emailed to participants before the meeting. Registration #913402-11.

Arlington Urban Forest Manager Vincent Verweij will provide an in-depth virtual overview of the county’s tree canopy at 3 p.m. Registration #913400-12.

Lubber Run 55+ Center will host a book exchange at 6 p.m. Participants can bring books in good condition to trade with other readers. Registration #913801-04.

Friday, March 28

The virtual “Fortunes in the Attic” program will offer participants a chance to have an item researched for its potential value while learning how to identify antiques. Community Arts Programmer Jennifer Droblyen will lead the 9 a.m. session. Photos must be emailed to sashton@arlingtonva.us before the program. Registration #913302-07.

A Spring Fling featuring games, snacks and social activities will run from 1 to 3 p.m. at Langston-Brown 55+ Center. Registration #913899-04.
