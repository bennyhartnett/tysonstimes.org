Alexandria Track Coaches Take Center Stage at Feb. 18 Event

The Alexandria Sportsman’s Club will bring together varsity track and field coaches from four local high schools during its monthly membership meeting Feb. 18 at the Old Dominion Boat Club.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Northern Virginia
Section
Local
Published
February 5, 2026
Topics
local, schools, arts, sports

Article Text

The Alexandria Sportsman’s Club will bring together varsity track and field coaches from four local high schools during its monthly membership meeting Feb. 18 at the Old Dominion Boat Club.

The roundtable will feature Adam Alderton of Alexandria City High School, Bob Artman of Bishop Ireton High School, Damian Walsh of Episcopal High School and Rob Watson of St. Stephen’s & St. Agnes School.

The club will also recognize local high school Athletes of the Month.

Doors open at 6:30 p.m., with a complimentary dinner available before the meeting begins at 7 p.m. The event will be held in the Old Dominion Boat Club Ballroom at 0 Prince Street in Old Town Alexandria.

Because space is limited and demand has increased, attendees must RSVP by emailing alexandriasportsmansclub@gmail.com. More information is available at alexandriavasports.org.
