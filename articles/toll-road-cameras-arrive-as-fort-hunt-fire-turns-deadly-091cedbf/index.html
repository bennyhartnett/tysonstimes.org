Toll Road Cameras Arrive as Fort Hunt Fire Turns Deadly

New license plate-reading cameras are being installed along the Dulles Toll Road, but officials say they are not intended to catch drivers avoiding tolls.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Reston
Section
Local
Published
April 15, 2026
Topics
local, reston, metro, public safety

Article Text

New license plate-reading cameras are being installed along the Dulles Toll Road, but officials say they are not intended to catch drivers avoiding tolls.

The Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority said the Flock cameras are part of a broader Virginia network used to identify stolen vehicles and assist with Amber Alerts.

In Fort Hunt, a person was hospitalized with life-threatening injuries after a house fire early Tuesday. Fairfax County firefighters responded around 2:40 a.m. to the 2100 block of Basset Street following a report of smoke. Two dogs were found dead, and investigators are working to determine what caused the fire.

Gov. Abigail Spanberger approved legislation Monday night ending Virginia’s centuries-old treatment of suicide as a common-law crime. The change comes a decade after Fairfax County firefighter Nicole Mittendorff died by suicide, an event that affected her family and prompted attention to the law.

Spanberger also amended dozens of bills and returned them to the General Assembly for reconsideration. Her proposed changes take a more moderate approach to several priorities advanced by the Democratic legislative majority, creating an early public divide between the governor and lawmakers.

The Potomac River has been named the nation’s most endangered river in a ranking by the nonprofit American Rivers. The organization pointed to January’s historic sewage spill, which released hundreds of millions of gallons of raw sewage, along with the growing concentration of data centers in the Washington area.

Reston Association announced three winners in its board election during Tuesday night’s annual members meeting. At-large director John Farrell and Apartment Owners’ Representative Margaret Perry won reelection to three-year terms. Georgina Cannan defeated two opponents to secure her first two-year term representing Lake Anne and Tall Oaks.

A free pop-up clinic will offer medical and vision services Saturday and Sunday, April 18-19, at Mubarak Mosque, 4555 Ahmadiyya Drive in Chantilly. The clinic is being organized by Remote Area Medical for people who might otherwise lack access to those services.

A new look at McLean’s housing market identifies the neighborhoods that fetched the highest prices in 2025. The northeastern Fairfax County community has long attracted politicians, diplomats, business leaders and professional athletes.

Wednesday’s forecast calls for sunshine and a high near 93 degrees, with southwest winds of 3 to 8 mph and gusts reaching 18 mph. Skies will turn partly cloudy overnight as temperatures fall to around 71 degrees, with southwest winds of 5 to 7 mph.
