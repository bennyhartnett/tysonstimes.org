Arlington’s 55+ Program Packs January With Music, Travel and Classes

Arlington’s 55+ Programs will offer a busy January schedule featuring live music, educational workshops, art activities, health guidance and regional excursions.

By Tysons Times Staff

Coverage area
Arlington
Section
Local
Published
January 8, 2025
Topics
local, arlington, development, arts

Article Text

Arlington’s 55+ Programs will offer a busy January schedule featuring live music, educational workshops, art activities, health guidance and regional excursions.

Most activities take place in person at Arlington’s 55+ Centers, although several are virtual. Participation requires a 55+ Pass, with annual fees beginning at $20. Registration is available online or by calling 703-228-4747.

On Monday, Jan. 13, Arlington Regional Master Naturalists will explain how to identify and remove invasive plants, harmful insects and pathogens. The discussion begins at 1 p.m. at Walter Reed 55+ Center and will be repeated at 5 p.m. Wednesday, Jan. 15, at Arlington Mill 55+ Center. Registration numbers are 912400-28 and 912400-29, respectively.

A virtual step-by-step painting demonstration with a Community Arts Programmer is scheduled for 10:30 a.m. Jan. 13. Registration number: 912303-02.

The 55+ Travel group will visit the Library of Congress in Washington on Tuesday, Jan. 14, for the “Collecting Memories: Treasures” exhibition. The trip costs $11 for Arlington residents and $13 for nonresidents. Registration number: 902401-06.

Also on Jan. 14, AARP speakers Bob Eiffert and Jane King will lead a HomeFit workshop about modifications that can help people remain in their homes. The program begins at 10:30 a.m. at Arlington Mill 55+ Center. Registration number: 912404-01.

Pianist Ken Schellenberg and bassist Chris Borsch will perform music spanning Duke Ellington’s career at 11:30 a.m. Jan. 14 at Lubber Run 55+ Center. Registration number: 912301-04.

The Arlington Senior Golf Club will hold its first meeting of 2025 at 11 a.m. Wednesday, Jan. 15, at Lubber Run 55+ Center. No registration is required. Members play at 26 Northern Virginia courses from April through October.

Elder law attorney Ed Zetlin will discuss the documents needed to organize an estate during a virtual presentation at 11 a.m. Jan. 15. Registration number: 912404-02.

A Virginia Hospital Center registered dietitian will present strategies for living well with diabetes at 10 a.m. Thursday, Jan. 16, at Arlington Mill 55+ Center. Topics include meal planning, portion control, exercise and blood-sugar monitoring. Registration number: 912500-04.

An Arlington park naturalist will examine the local and global effects of invasive species—from plants and animals to microscopic bacteria—during a virtual session at 11 a.m. Jan. 16. Registration number: 912400-30.

At the same time, Virginia Cooperative Extension Master Gardener Lynn Berry will discuss houseplant care at Langston-Brown 55+ Center. Registration number: 912401-01.

“Artist Cookbook,” a virtual class about making art materials from household items, begins at 9 a.m. Jan. 16. Community Arts Programmer Jennifer Droblyen will lead the session. Registration number: 912302-11.

Community Arts Programmer Jim Halloran will explore the history and context of three artists during a virtual program at 10:30 a.m. Friday, Jan. 17. A short demonstration of one artist’s process and a discussion will follow. Registration number: 912302-07.

Pianist Valerie Welsh will perform in a winter piano lounge at 1 p.m. Jan. 17 at Aurora Hills 55+ Center. Registration number: 912301-06.

Genealogist Eileen Bogdanoff will introduce research using primary sources, cemetery records, family documents, DNA services and other tools at 11:30 a.m. Tuesday, Jan. 21, at Lubber Run 55+ Center. Registration number: 912402-02.

Volunteer George Cecchetti will lead an opera-appreciation program on Giuseppe Verdi’s “Rigoletto” at 1:30 p.m. Wednesday, Jan. 22, at Lubber Run 55+ Center. He will explain the story and provide background on the composer. Registration number: 912300-04.

The 55+ Travel group will head to Walmart in Sterling and then visit Dulles Town Center for additional shopping and dining on Jan. 22. The trip costs $15 for Arlington residents and $17 for nonresidents. Registration number: 902401-07.

Aurora Hills 55+ Center will screen “The World’s Fastest Indian” at 12:30 p.m. Jan. 22. Registration number: 912804-05.

Ashley Gomez, director of Lubber Run 55+ Center, will present a program about Arlington history at 1:30 p.m. Jan. 22. Registration number: 912402-07.

A two-part advance-care-planning series begins at 11 a.m. Jan. 22 at Langston-Brown 55+ Center with guidance on preparing a health-care advance directive. The second session, at 11 a.m. Wednesday, Jan. 29, will provide an opportunity to meet individually with a certified volunteer. Attendance at the first session is required to participate in the second. Registration number: 912404-03.

Peter North, an outreach specialist at the Library of Virginia in Richmond, will give a virtual presentation about Virginia after the Civil War at 3 p.m. Thursday, Jan. 23. Registration number: 912400-18.

Gomez will also lead a virtual presentation about Peru at 1 p.m. Jan. 23 as part of a series on countries in Central America. Registration number: 912400-39.

Musicians of all experience levels can join an old-time and string-band jam at 5 p.m. Jan. 23 at Lubber Run 55+ Center. Volunteer Fred Winter, co-founder of New York City’s Old Time Music Project, will lead the event. Participants may bring a banjo, guitar, fiddle, mandolin or dulcimer. Registration number: 912304-19.

Cecchetti, a former Kennedy Space Center employee, will recount NASA’s space shuttle program from its beginning through its conclusion at 2 p.m. Friday, Jan. 24, at Langston-Brown 55+ Center. Registration number: 912400-33.

The month’s listed excursions conclude Saturday, Jan. 25, with a 55+ Travel visit to the National Law Enforcement Museum in Washington. The museum covers 300 years of history and holds 25,000 artifacts. The trip costs $36 for Arlington residents and $40 for nonresidents. Participants may bring lunch or purchase a snack at the museum café. Registration number: 902401-08.
