More Than 400 Fairfax Students Claim Prestigious IB Honors

More than 400 Fairfax County Public Schools students earned International Baccalaureate credentials in 2025, completing demanding academic or career-focused programs at schools across...

By Tysons Times Staff

Article Text

More than 400 Fairfax County Public Schools students earned International Baccalaureate credentials in 2025, completing demanding academic or career-focused programs at schools across the county.

Nearly 400 students received the IB Diploma, while 76 earned the IB Career-related Certificate.

The two-year Diploma Program requires juniors and seniors to study six subject areas and complete internal and external assessments, exams, a Theory of Knowledge course, a creativity, activity and service project, and a 4,000-word extended essay.

Diploma recipients attended Annandale, Edison, Justice, Lewis, Marshall, Mount Vernon, South Lakes and Robinson Secondary schools.

The two-year Career-related Program combines IB coursework with career-focused study. Participants complete at least two IB courses, a two-year career-related course sequence, assessments and exams, personal and professional skills coursework, service learning, world-language development, and a reflective project examining an ethical dilemma in their chosen field.

Career-related Certificate recipients attended Mount Vernon and South Lakes high schools.

Both programs are intended to prepare internationally minded and compassionate students for higher education and the workforce.
